Spot Copy Trading Platform

Followers automatically mirror an experienced Lead Trader — when the lead opens, modifies or closes a position, the same trade is executed in every follower's account, scaled to their allocation.

Laravel NextJS Redis Nginx PHP 8.4 MySQL

Role

Backend Developer

Stack

Laravel · Next.js

Pattern

1 lead → N followers

Overview

Copy trading lets a user pick an experienced Lead Trader and follow them. When the lead opens, modifies or closes a position, the platform automatically executes the same trade in the followers' accounts, sized according to each follower's allocated investment and copy settings.

Crucially, each follower keeps full ownership of their own funds and takes their own profits and losses — the lead never touches follower money. The lead earns a profit-sharing commission out of the followers' realized profits.

I built the backend replication engine: the fan-out from one lead action to many follower orders, the proportional sizing, and the commission settlement.

Key Features

  • Lead trader profiles — public performance stats so followers can choose who to copy.
  • Follow with an allocation — a follower commits a specific amount, not their whole balance.
  • Automatic mirroring — open, modify and close events are replicated to every active follower.
  • Proportional sizing — each follower's order is scaled to their allocation, not copied 1:1.
  • Profit-share commission — the lead earns an agreed cut of realized follower profit.
  • Follower controls — stop copying, set risk limits, or withdraw at any time; funds stay in the follower's own account.

What I Did

  • Built the replication engine that fans a single lead action out to all of that lead's active followers.
  • Implemented proportional order sizing based on each follower's allocated investment and copy settings.
  • Implemented the profit-sharing commission — calculated on realized profit only, and settled to the lead.
  • Kept follower funds isolated: a follower's balance is only ever debited for their own mirrored trade.
  • Pushed the fan-out through queue workers so a lead with many followers doesn't block the lead's own request.
  • Designed the APIs backing the Next.js frontend and the mobile app.

Challenges & How I Solved Them

1

Fan-out under load

One lead closing a position can mean hundreds of follower orders at once. The replication runs as queued jobs per follower, so the lead's own trade returns immediately and the copies are processed by workers in parallel.

2

Followers who can't fill

A follower may not have enough balance, or may have stopped copying mid-trade. Each follower's copy is evaluated independently — one failure is recorded and skipped rather than aborting the whole fan-out.

3

Commission only on real profit

A lead should not earn commission on a position that lost money, and should not be paid twice for the same profit. Commission is computed from realized profit at close, per follower, per position.

4

Duplicate replication

A retried queue job must not open a second copy of the same trade. Copy jobs are idempotent — keyed on the lead's action, so re-running one is a no-op.
